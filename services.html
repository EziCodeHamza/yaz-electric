Questions Dublin customers ask us

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often.

How much does a house rewire cost in Dublin?

It depends on the size, age and condition of the house — which is why every rewire starts with a survey and a clear, itemised quote. As a general guide, a typical three-bed semi-detached home takes approximately 3–5 days to rewire, though the exact duration is confirmed after assessing the property. The price agreed at survey is what you pay, unless the scope of the job changes.

Do I need an electrical certificate for my rental property?

Best practice in Ireland is a full periodic inspection at least every five years, and letting agents, insurers and RTB inspections routinely ask to see it. We inspect, test and issue a clear, coded report with a plain-English summary — and a quote for any repairs it uncovers, if you want one.

Can YAZ help with the SEAI grant for EV charger installation?

Yes — YAZ can assist customers with the SEAI home charger grant process and provide the necessary documentation where applicable. Grant eligibility and approval are subject to SEAI's own requirements, including property type, charger specification and other criteria. We'd recommend checking the current SEAI website for the latest terms before applying.

What does a periodic inspection actually involve?

A full visual inspection plus live testing of the installation — circuits, RCDs, earthing and bonding. You get a detailed report with findings clearly coded, and we walk you through what each one means in plain English. Typically takes a few hours for an average home.

How quickly can you respond to an emergency?

Emergency callouts are available subject to our current availability. Submit an urgent enquiry via the contact form and we'll respond as quickly as we can. Response times will vary depending on workload and location at the time. For immediate life-safety concerns, please contact the Emergency Services or your ESB Networks operator first.

Do you cover areas outside Dublin city?

Yes. Our main service area covers Dublin City, all Dublin postal districts (D1–D24), North, South and West Dublin, and the surrounding suburbs. We also work across County Kildare, County Meath and County Wicklow. Other surrounding areas may be considered depending on the size and nature of the job — get in touch and we'll be straight with you about whether we can help.
