Questions Dublin customers ask us

Straight answers to the questions we hear most often.

How much does a house rewire cost in Dublin?

It depends on the size, age and condition of the house — which is why every rewire starts with a free survey and a fixed, itemised quote. As a guide, a typical three-bed semi takes three to five days. The quote we agree at survey is the price you pay, unless you change the job.

Do I need an electrical certificate for my rental property?

Best practice in Ireland is a full periodic inspection at least every five years, and letting agents, insurers and RTB inspections routinely ask to see it. We inspect, test and issue a clear, coded report with a plain-English summary — and a quote for any repairs it uncovers, if you want one.

Is the SEAI grant still available for EV charger installation?

Yes. The SEAI home charger grant is €300 (2026) for a smart charger on the SEAI register, installed by a Safe Electric registered contractor for a homeowner with off-street parking. We handle the grant application as part of the installation, so you don't chase paperwork.

What does a periodic inspection actually involve?

A full visual inspection plus live testing of the installation — circuits, RCDs, earthing and bonding. You get a detailed report with findings clearly coded, and we walk you through what each one means in plain English. Typically takes a few hours for an average home.

How quickly can you get to an emergency?

Typically 1–2 hours across Dublin for genuine emergencies. Call 01 234 5678 — we'll confirm a time on the spot and talk you through anything you should do in the meantime.

Do you cover areas outside Dublin city?

Yes. We work across Dublin city and county — Fingal, South Dublin and Dún Laoghaire–Rathdown — plus parts of Kildare, Meath and Wicklow. If you're unsure whether we cover your area, call and we'll tell you straight.

Not sure which service you need?

Describe the job on the phone and we'll tell you exactly what's involved, what it costs and when we can do it.
