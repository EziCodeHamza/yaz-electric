An electrical contractor Dublin can depend on

YAZ Electrical Solution Ltd is a Dublin-based electrical contractor serving homeowners, landlords and businesses across Greater Dublin, Kildare, Meath and Wicklow.

Built on reliability, quality and honesty

As a newly established electrical contractor powered by experienced, fully qualified electricians, YAZ Electrical Solution Ltd was founded to set a high benchmark for domestic and commercial electrical services across Greater Dublin. From day one, our commitment has been absolute safety, precise workmanship, honest pricing and customer satisfaction.

Every job is carried out by qualified electricians working to the current edition of the I.S. 10101 wiring rules. We issue certificates on every applicable job and provide clear, itemised quotes before a single cable is pulled.

The goal is simple: to be a trusted local electrical company that customers come back to — and recommend — for installations, repairs, maintenance and electrical solutions across Greater Dublin and the surrounding counties.

The values we work by

Four principles that shape how we quote, how we work and how we follow up.

Safety first

If a job isn't safe, it doesn't get signed off. Isolating, testing and certifying are non-negotiable — on every callout, at every price point.

Honest pricing

Clear, itemised quotes before work starts. If we find something extra mid-job, we stop and talk to you before spending a cent more.

Quality workmanship

Floors protected, chases filled, dust hoovered and rubbish removed at the end of every day. The site looks the way we'd want our own home left.

Clear communication

You'll know who's coming, when, what they'll do and what it costs. Plain English, no jargon, and a straight answer to every question.

The paperwork that matters

Anyone can say they're qualified. Here's what backs it up — and what you should expect from any electrician you hire in Ireland.

  • Certified to I.S. 10101 — the current Irish wiring rules, applied on every installation where applicable
  • Qualified electricians — practical experience and a strong understanding of Irish electrical standards
  • Certificates on completion — you get the paperwork for your records, your insurer or your tenant file
  • Clear, itemised quotes — agreed in writing before work starts, with any unforeseen changes discussed before proceeding

How we keep your records straight

Good paperwork protects you as much as it protects us. Here's what you get with every applicable YAZ job:

Completion certificates

Issued for new and altered circuits, as required under the Irish wiring rules — proof the work meets the standard for your insurer or buyer.

Inspection reports

Clear, coded findings for periodic inspections and landlord certificates — easy for letting agents and the RTB to read, with a plain-English summary for you.

PAT logs & maintenance records

Portable appliance testing registers and maintenance histories for commercial customers, kept tidy and handed over in a format you can actually use.
