An electrical contractor Dublin can depend on

YAZ Electrical Solution Ltd is a Dublin-based electrical contractor serving homeowners, landlords and businesses across the city and surrounding counties.

Built by electricians, run like it

YAZ started with a simple frustration: too many Dublin homeowners and business owners couldn't find an electrician who would quote honestly, turn up on time and leave the place cleaner than they found it. So we built that company ourselves.

Every YAZ electrician is qualified, Safe Electric registered and vetted before they ever set foot on a customer's site. We work to the current edition of the I.S. 10101 wiring rules, we issue certificates on every job, and we quote fixed, itemised prices before a single cable is pulled.

Today YAZ handles everything from single-socket repairs to full house rewires, commercial fit-outs and landlord compliance programmes — with the same rule on every job, whatever the size: if it's not safe, it's not done.

The values we work by

Four rules that decide how we quote, how we work and how we follow up.

Safety first

If a job isn't safe, it doesn't get signed off. Isolating, testing and certifying are non-negotiable — on every callout, at every price point.

Straight pricing

Fixed, itemised quotes before work starts. If we find something extra mid-job, we stop and talk to you before spending a cent more.

Tidy workmanship

Floors protected, chases filled, dust hoovered and rubbish removed at the end of every day. The site looks the way we'd want our own home left.

Clear communication

You'll know who's coming, when, what they'll do and what it costs. Plain English, no jargon, and a straight answer to every question.

The paperwork that matters

Anyone can say they're qualified. Here's what backs it up — and what you should expect from any electrician you hire in Ireland.

  • Safe Electric registered — the register of electrical contractors in Ireland, so certificates we issue carry the weight they should
  • Fully insured — public liability cover on every job, domestic and commercial
  • Certified to I.S. 10101 — the current Irish wiring rules, on every installation
  • Qualified and vetted — QQI Level 6 Advanced Craft qualified electricians, with apprentices trained in-house
  • Certificates on completion — you get the paperwork for your records, your insurer or your tenant file

How we keep your records straight

Good paperwork protects you as much as it protects us. Here's what you get with every YAZ job:

Completion certificates

Issued for new and altered circuits, as required under the Irish wiring rules — proof the work meets the standard for your insurer or buyer.

Inspection reports

Clear, coded findings for periodic inspections and landlord certificates — easy for letting agents and the RTB to read, with a plain-English summary for you.

PAT logs & maintenance records

Portable appliance testing registers and maintenance histories for commercial customers, kept tidy and handed over in a format you can actually use.
