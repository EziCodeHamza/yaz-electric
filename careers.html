Qualified Electrician

Hiring now Full-time Dublin & surrounding areas

What you'll do

  • Domestic and commercial installations — rewires, fuse board upgrades, fit-outs
  • Testing, inspection and fault finding to I.S. 10101
  • Issuing completion certificates and reports for your own work
  • Dealing directly with customers — we expect our electricians to be presentable and straight-talking
  • Keeping the van and tools in good order

What we're looking for

  • QQI Level 6 Advanced Craft Certificate in Electrical (or equivalent)
  • Safe Electric registered, or eligible and willing to register
  • Full, clean driving licence
  • Own tools and a professional manner on site
  • Experience across domestic and commercial work preferred
  • Pride in tidy workmanship — it's how we got our reputation

No current apprenticeships advertised — but we hire apprentices through our in-house programme when places open.
