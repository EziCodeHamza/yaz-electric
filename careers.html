Qualified Electrician

Hiring now Full-time Greater Dublin & surrounding areas

What you'll do

  • Carry out electrical installations across domestic and commercial projects
  • Carry out maintenance, repairs, testing and fault-finding to I.S. 10101
  • Issue completion certificates and documentation for completed work
  • Deal directly with customers in a professional, clear and courteous manner
  • Maintain high standards of workmanship and site tidiness on every job

What we're looking for

  • Relevant electrical qualifications
  • Practical experience in domestic and/or commercial electrical work
  • Strong understanding of Irish electrical standards and safe working practices
  • Full driving licence is an advantage (not mandatory)
  • Own tools preferred
  • Pride in tidy, high-quality workmanship

No apprentice positions currently advertised — but YAZ is open to supporting apprentices as the business grows.

How to apply

Send your CV to the address below with the subject line exactly as written — it makes sure your application reaches the right inbox.

TO:  careers@yazelectrical.ie
SUBJECT:  Qualified Electrician Application – YAZ Electrical Solution

Along with your CV, please include a short introduction outlining your qualifications, electrical experience, availability and any relevant certifications. We read every application and aim to reply to all of them.
